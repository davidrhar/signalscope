SignalScope Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This explains how the SignalScope Android app handles your data. SignalScope is published by Bonufied Pte Ltd ("Bonufied", "we", "us"), a company registered in Singapore, and this policy is written in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

In short: SignalScope keeps what it measures on your phone. Nothing leaves your phone unless you turn on Contribute to the shared coverage map, and even then what is sent is coarse, anonymous summary data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app records on your phone

To work out why your mobile data drops, SignalScope records the following in a database stored on your device:

  • Mobile network state: signal strength and quality, network type (4G, 5G and so on), frequency band, mobile network operator, which mobile mast (cell) is serving you, and the state of each SIM.
  • Connection state: whether you are on Wi-Fi or mobile data, and whether the connection actually works, including the results of small connection and speed tests.
  • Whether a call or audio is in progress: only whether one is happening, so the app avoids running tests that would interrupt it. It never records who you call or what you play.
  • Approximate location, if you grant location permission. The app never stores your exact latitude and longitude. Each position is rounded to a hexagonal area before it is saved. The smallest of these areas is about 65 m across.

Please treat this data as sensitive. Even rounded, a timestamped history of areas and serving masts is a record of where you have been. This record is kept whether or not you grant location permission, because the serving mast is itself a movement history.

This data stays on your phone. We do not receive it and cannot see it.

Like any internet service, these providers see your device's IP address when your phone connects to them. Our own server does not record IP addresses, set cookies or assign identifiers to contributions.

How we use shared contributions

Contributions are combined with those from other phones to build a public map comparing mobile network coverage across areas. An area is published only once at least three separate contributions cover it. Contributions are kept as received for up to 30 days and then deleted, leaving only the combined map. Because contributions carry no identifier, they cannot be linked to you or to each other, and we cannot pick out any one person's contribution.

We do not sell data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Phone state: to read signal, network and SIM status. The app does not read your phone number, calls or messages.
  • Location (precise and approximate): Android needs this for detailed cell information and to place measurements on the map. Positions are rounded before they are saved.
  • Network access and network state: to see which connection is in use and to run connection tests.
  • Foreground service, notifications and start at boot: so measurement can keep running, with a visible notification, while the app is not on screen.
  • Shizuku (optional): if you have installed the Shizuku app and grant access, SignalScope can read some additional radio details. It is not required.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Contribute is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in the app.
  • To delete everything SignalScope has recorded, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.
  • We cannot delete or return a contribution you have already sent, because nothing in it identifies you or your phone.

Children

SignalScope is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

Security

All data the app sends to our server uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Our server's storage is encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change is released.
