✕ What SignalScope cannot do
Hard Android limits, not missing features. Please don’t file them as bugs.
  • No ICMP ping · isReachable() uses TCP/7 and lies
  • No traceroute · raw sockets need root
  • No modem diagnostics · /dev/diag, AT, RIL are vendor-only
  • No band or cell locking · needs a UICC-signed app
  • No forced handover · re-anchor is the strongest lever
